An older GPT beat the newer one by 29 points

GPT-5.6 Sol scored 77.3; the newer GPT-6 Sol scored 48.7. The newer model didn't make more errors. It wrote shorter answers.

3 November 2026

On 3 November we added two earlier GPT models, run on Codex with the same settings as the others. The older one came out far ahead.

  • GPT-5.6 Sol: 77.3. GPT-6 Sol: 48.7. The gap is 28.6 points (95% interval 24.2 to 33.2), and the older model was ahead on 57 of the 59 questions both answered.
  • It's not more mistakes. GPT-6 Sol wrote shorter answers (a median of about 200 words against about 310, with less thinking) that covered fewer of the sources and rulings the rubrics ask for: 48% of the requirements, against 77%.
  • Claude went the other way. Claude Opus 5.5 is 21.0 points above Claude Opus 4.8 (17.4 to 24.9). Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Sonnet 4.6 are not clearly apart.

GPT-5.6 Sol now sits fifth on the board, not clearly apart from Claude Fable 5.1 and Gemini 4 Argon. On one question, OpenAI's safety system flagged every attempt by both models partway through; those answers score 0, as every failed answer does (see the notes on the leaderboard).
