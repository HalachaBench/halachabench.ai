Gemini 4 Carbon climbs to second

82.3, four points above Gemini 4 Argon and not clearly apart from Claude Fable 5.2. It thinks far longer than any other model, about 69,000 tokens per answer.

13 November 2026

Google's Gemini 4 Carbon scores 82.3 (95% interval 78.4 – 86.0), second on the board.

  • Clearly ahead of its sibling. Carbon is 4.1 points above Gemini 4 Argon (1.8 to 6.4), and clearly ahead of GPT-6 Astra and every model below it.
  • Not clearly apart from the leader. Claude Fable 5.2 is ahead by 3.5 points, but the 95% interval runs from −0.3 to 7.1.
  • It just thinks… a lot. A median of about 69,000 output tokens and 13 minutes per answer, against about 14,000 for Argon and about 6,000 for Fable 5.2. Two attempts at one question timed out after 30 minutes and were rerun under the registered retry rule; all 180 answers are scored.

As the chart from before Carbon's run shows, more thinking hasn't generally bought a better answer: Fable 5.1 and GPT-6 Astra reached the top with a few thousand tokens.

During Carbon's grading the judge service began sending a new kind of status message. A recorded amendment let the harness accept it, all of Carbon's answers were graded again from the start, and no other model's grades changed.
