Which semicha paper is hardest for AI?

Chuppah v'Kiddushin and Niddah were the hardest papers for nearly every model. Mitzvot HaTeluyot BaAretz was the easiest.

2 November 2026

Each of the five Cheshvan 5787 papers has 12 questions. Splitting the first results by paper shows nearly the same order for every model.

  • Hardest: Chuppah v'Kiddushin and Niddah. Even the leader at the time, Claude Fable 5.1, scored 73 and 77 there.
  • Easiest: Mitzvot HaTeluyot BaAretz. The top models scored in the 80s, and most weaker ones did best here too.
  • The order holds across ability. Strong and weak models find the same papers hard, which suggests the difficulty lies in the material and the rubrics, not in one model's blind spot.

Twelve questions per paper is a small sample, so these differences are descriptive. The chart shows the results of 1 November; the full leaderboard has every model added since.
