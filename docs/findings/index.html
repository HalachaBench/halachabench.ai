What we've found

Each result we've published, with the data behind it. Newest first.

HalachaBench 13 November 2026

Gemini 4 Carbon climbs to second

82.3, four points above Gemini 4 Argon and not clearly apart from Claude Fable 5.2. It thinks far longer than any other model, about 69,000 tokens per answer.
HashkafaBench 12 November 2026

Tell the AI you're Sephardi

The models carry a detailed map of where Sephardi practice departs from Ashkenazi practice, and apply it when told who is asking: more lenient overall, but only where the traditions differ.
HalachaBench 10 November 2026

Claude Fable 5.2 takes first place

85.8, the highest score yet and clearly ahead of every model then on the board. Better than Fable 5.1 on all five papers; even its worst attempts beat 5.1's average.
Values 9 November 2026

AI says Aharon HaKohen's white lie is allowed. It won't tell one.

13 models gave a halachic ruling, then said what they'd do themselves. Asked twice, the ruling repeats 75% of the time; their own choice matches it only 41% of the time.
HashkafaBench 9 November 2026

Which poskim are the models closest to?

Claude and the Geminis rule near R. Ovadia Yosef and R. Shlomo Zalman Auerbach; the GPT models cluster with the Shulchan Aruch and Rema, and rarely cite a 20th-century posek.
HashkafaBench 6 November 2026

Is your AI stringent or lenient?

13 models, 288 everyday questions. Claude's models rule most leniently; GPT, Gemini and Grok can't be told apart. On bein adam lachavero, every lab agrees.
HalachaBench 6 November 2026

Meta's Muse models take the exam

Muse Wonder Full scored 60.8, just behind Gemini 3.8 Flash. Muse Wonder scored 48.2, Muse Spark 1.3 36.2, and the lightest, Muse Ember, 15.3.
HalachaBench 3 November 2026

An older GPT beat the newer one by 29 points

GPT-5.6 Sol scored 77.3; the newer GPT-6 Sol scored 48.7. The newer model didn't make more errors. It wrote shorter answers.
HalachaBench 2 November 2026

Checks on the Grok results

Grok 4.7 scored 29.9, far below the leaders. We checked the result five ways and it holds up. Its answers are short.
HalachaBench 2 November 2026

Which semicha paper is hardest for AI?

Chuppah v'Kiddushin and Niddah were the hardest papers for nearly every model. Mitzvot HaTeluyot BaAretz was the easiest.
HalachaBench 1 November 2026

We gave AI a semicha exam

Real Chief Rabbinate semicha questions, in Hebrew, with no books and no internet. The best model scored 80 out of 100; many scored under 11.
