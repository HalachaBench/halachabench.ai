Findings HalachaBench 6 November 2026

Meta's Muse models take the exam

Muse Wonder Full scored 60.8, just behind Gemini 3.8 Flash. Muse Wonder scored 48.2, Muse Spark 1.3 36.2, and the lightest, Muse Ember, 15.3.

On 6 November we added three of Meta's Muse models, run through Meta's Muse Code CLI with its web, shell and file-writing tools switched off and every run monitored live. Muse Ember, Meta's lightest Muse model, followed on 9 November, the day it was released.

Model Score 95% interval
Muse Wonder Full 60.8 56.4 – 65.2
Muse Wonder 48.2 43.4 – 53.1
Muse Spark 1.3 36.2 32.2 – 40.4
Muse Ember 15.3 12.0 – 18.8
Muse Glimmer 30B (open weights, run locally) 10.5 7.6 – 13.7
  • Each step down the family costs about 12 points. Muse Wonder Full is 12.5 points above Muse Wonder, and Muse Wonder 12.0 above Muse Spark 1.3; both gaps are clear.
  • Muse Wonder Full places just behind Gemini 3.8 Flash (not clearly apart) and clearly behind Claude Opus 5.5.
  • A coding assistant's habits show. About 25 of 540 Muse answers format their citations as Markdown links to placeholder file paths or web addresses. The citations themselves are ordinary, and the answers were graded as written.
