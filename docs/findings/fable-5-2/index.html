Claude Fable 5.2 takes first place

85.8, the highest score yet and clearly ahead of every model then on the board. Better than Fable 5.1 on all five papers; even its worst attempts beat 5.1's average.

10 November 2026

Claude Fable 5.2 scores 85.8 (95% interval 82.7 – 88.5), the highest score on HalachaBench.

  • It beats its predecessor clearly. Fable 5.1 scored 79.7; Fable 5.2 is ahead by 6.0 points (3.0 to 9.1), and better on all five semicha papers.
  • Its floor is 5.1's average. Taking only each question's worst of three attempts, Fable 5.2 scores 79.8, above Fable 5.1's mean.
  • It was clearly ahead of every other model at the time: of Gemini 4 Argon by 7.5 points (3.7 to 11.2) and of GPT-5.6 Sol by 8.5 (4.2 to 13.4). Three days later Gemini 4 Carbon came within 3.5 points, not clearly apart.

It ran like every other model on the board: Claude Code CLI, every tool switched off, effort high, on the same 60 questions, graded by the same sealed rubrics and judge. One note for fairness: the rubrics were drafted by its predecessor, Claude Fable 5.1, and a rubric written by a model may favour that model's way of answering. The second judge and the rabbis' grading will show how much this matters.
