Checks on the Grok results

Grok 4.7 scored 29.9, far below the leaders. We checked the result five ways and it holds up. Its answers are short.

2 November 2026

Grok 4.7 scores well below the leaders, so its result was checked more than any other. In short: its answers are short, and the low score holds up when the grading or the rubrics are changed.

  • Its answers were complete. All 180 answers finished normally, in Hebrew, with no output limit set and none cut off; reasoning effort was high on every one.
  • It writes short answers. Grok 4.7 thought at length (a median of about 18,000 reasoning tokens per answer) but wrote a median of 213 words, against 368 for Claude Sonnet 4.6 and 664 for Gemini 4 Argon. The rubrics give points for each source, step and ruling an answer gives, so a short answer that is right on the bottom line still scores low.
  • A blind second grader agreed with the judge. Claude Opus 5.5, without knowing which model wrote which answer, re-graded 30 answers and agreed with GPT-6 Astra on 88% of criterion decisions, equally for both models. Astra was not harder on Grok.
  • The rubrics Grok disputed don't explain its score. Grok 4.7 reviewed all 60 sealed rubrics and asked to change or remove 405 of their 661 criteria. Scored without the criteria it disputed, its score goes down, not up.
Scored on Grok 4.7 Rank Gemini 4 Argon Rank
All criteria 29.9 10 78.2 2
Without the 274 criteria Grok said should go 28.5 10 76.8 2

Ranks among the 22 models scored on 2 November.

  • Most of Grok's critique did not hold up. Each point that would change a grade was checked against the sources on Sefaria. 24 were confirmed as real rubric errors, in 29 criteria; 11 were refuted. Scored without those 29 criteria, Grok 4.7 stays at 29.9, and no model moves by more than 0.7 points.
  • Grok as the judge gives the same result. Grading with the same sealed rubrics, Grok 4.7 agreed with Astra on 92% of 1,937 criterion decisions and did not score its own answers higher.

None of this changes the scores: the rubrics stay as sealed. Any rubric error found is corrected for every model at once, in the next version.
