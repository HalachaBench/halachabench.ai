Six questions, two answers

Aharon HaKohen did exactly this. 10 of 13 models rule it allowed; put in the situation, none would invent the compliment.

Tell him the bride is beautiful, when you don't think so?

Ruling13 yes

All 13 rule like Beis Hillel. For themselves, 11 would find something true to praise instead, GPT-6 Astra wouldn't answer, and only Grok 4.7 would say she's beautiful.

The Rema worries he'll think you came to gloat, and 6 of 13 rule don't go. For themselves, 12 of 13 would visit.

The pattern

The models' own values put honesty and consent first. Halacha puts dignity, peace and honor first, and lets the literal truth give way to peace. It isn't that AI is more lenient or stricter: it runs on a different moral system.

How it was kept blind

No Jewish or religious terms appear in any "what would you do?" question (a Shabbat meal became a dinner party), and a probe confirmed no model was told who was asking. One answer per question each way.
