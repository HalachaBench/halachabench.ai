How well do AI models know halacha? We gave them the real semicha exams of Israel's Chief Rabbinate: five papers of the Cheshvan 5787 sitting, in Hebrew, with no seforim and no internet, three attempts at every question. Every answer was graded against a rubric sealed before grading began, and the scores are set out here, with the measure of their doubt beside them.

The studies

Stringent or lenient. Asked everyday questions in seven areas of halacha, Claude's models rule most leniently, and GPT, Gemini and Grok cannot be told apart. Within one lab the spread can exceed the gap between labs. Halacha against its own values. Asked the same halachic question twice, a model gives the same ruling 75% of the time. Asked what it would do itself, it matches its own ruling only 41% of the time.

When the asker is Sephardi. rulings grow 0.06 more lenient on average, almost all of it in Shabbat, Yom Tov, kashrut and fasts, where the traditions differ. The hardest paper. Chuppah v'Kiddushin and Niddah, for nearly every model; Mitzvot HaTeluyot BaAretz is the easiest. Lately. Gemini 4 Carbon climbs to second; Tell the AI you're Sephardi; Claude Fable 5.2 takes first place; AI says Aharon HaKohen's white lie is allowed. It won't tell one. Every finding
