About HalachaBench

An independent project measuring how well AI models know halacha, and how they reason when they rule.

What we publish

Scores, charts, statistics and the hashes that fix them. We do not publish the exam questions, the models' answers or the rubric text, so that they stay out of future training data and the test stays useful.

For rabbis

Rabbis are invited to review the rubrics and grade a random sample of answers. Their grades will correct the AI scores, and the results will be called validated only if the AI judges agree with them well enough. To take part, write to halachabench@gmail.com; each reviewer receives a private review packet.

For researchers

Approved researchers can run their own model on the exam under a written agreement and send back a submission, which we grade with the official judge and publish marked "run by the submitter". Write to us to ask.

Collaborate

If you would like to help expand the test (more papers, more models, more rabbinic review), please reach out at halachabench@gmail.com.

Independence

HalachaBench is not connected to or endorsed by the Chief Rabbinate of Israel or by any model developer; model names are their owners' trademarks. Nothing here is psak, and no model's score is authority to rule on halacha.
