Every rubric position in HashkafaBench names the authorities who hold it, so a posek can be placed in the same space as
the models. We looked three ways, from most to least dependent on the judge model. All three are descriptive.

## 1. Rulings

{{chart:poskim-map||Models and poskim in the same ruling space}}

The Claude models and the two Geminis sit on the side of R. Ovadia Yosef and R. Shlomo Zalman Auerbach; the GPT models
cluster with the Shulchan Aruch, Rema and Shemiras Shabbos K'hilchasah.

| Authority (questions) | Closest model (mean gap) | Farthest model (mean gap) |
|---|---|---|
| Shulchan Aruch (101) | GPT-6 Sol 0.33 | GPT-6 Luna 0.51 |
| Rema (67) | GPT-6 Sol 0.28 | Claude Haiku 5.5 0.53 |
| Mishnah Berurah (33) | GPT-6 Astra 0.18 | Claude Haiku 5.5 0.53 |
| R. Moshe Feinstein (31) | Claude Opus 5.5 0.34 | GPT-6 Luna 0.51 |
| R. Shlomo Zalman Auerbach (15) | Claude Fable 5.1 0.32 | GPT-6 Astra 0.61 |
| R. Ovadia Yosef (12) | Claude Fable 5.1 0.22 | GPT-6 Luna 0.44 |

A caution: R. Shlomo Zalman Auerbach and R. Ovadia Yosef appear mostly at lenient anchors in the rubrics, so "closest
to R. Ovadia Yosef" largely means "lenient where he is lenient". The intervals are wide.

## 2. Citations (no judge model)

{{chart:citations-map||Whom the models cite}}

Every model cites the Shulchan Aruch, Rema and Mishnah Berurah. The labs differ in whether they go further:

| Authority | Claude | Gemini | GPT | Grok |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Shulchan Aruch | 83% | 84% | 73% | 67% |
| Mishnah Berurah | 59% | 51% | 32% | 36% |
| R. Moshe Feinstein | 30% | 33% | 4% | 14% |
| R. Shlomo Zalman Auerbach | 16% | 16% | 1% | 5% |
| Shemiras Shabbos K'hilchasah | 15% | 15% | 2% | 10% |

Claude and Gemini name 20th-century poskim in roughly one answer in six; the GPT models almost never do. R. Ovadia
Yosef is almost never cited, consistent with an Ashkenazi asker.

## 3. Style (no judge model)

{{chart:style||Which models write alike}}

By Burrows's Delta on their most frequent words, the models cluster by lab: GPT in one branch, Claude and the Geminis
in another, Grok nearest Claude.
