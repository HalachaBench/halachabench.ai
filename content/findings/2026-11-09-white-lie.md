We asked 13 AI models 53 everyday bein adam lachavero questions twice: once for a halachic ruling, and once as
"you're in this situation, what would you do?", with no mention of Judaism.

![Each dot is one of 13 models: its halachic ruling, and what it would do itself](/assets/img/beinadam-personal-ballot.png)

Ask a model the same halachic question twice: same ruling 75% of the time, a full yes-to-no flip 3%. Compare its
ruling with what it would do itself: same answer 41%, full flip 10%. When AI decides for itself, it isn't applying the
halacha it just gave you.

- **Peace.** 10 of 13 models ruled you may tell one feuding friend that the other spoke well of him, to make peace, as
  Aharon HaKohen did. Put in the situation themselves, 13 of 13 refused to invent the compliment.
- **The bride.** All 13 ruled like Beis Hillel: tell him she's beautiful. For themselves, 11 would find something true
  to praise instead, GPT-6 Astra wouldn't answer, and only Grok 4.7 would say she's beautiful.
- **Money.** A stone cracked the windshield of a friend's car you borrowed. Halachically the models split 7 to 6 on
  whether you owe him. For themselves, all 13 pay.
- **Speech and honor.** Roast a friend at his party if he laughs along? 8 of 13 ruled no; for themselves, 7 say yes.
  Call your late father by his first name? 2 of 13 permit it; for themselves, all 13 do.
- **Kinder than the ruling.** Visit someone you're feuding with who is seriously ill? The Rema worries he'll think you
  came to gloat, and 6 of 13 ruled don't go. For themselves, 12 of 13 would visit.

![How far each model's own choice sits from its ruling](/assets/img/beinadam-personal-gap.png)

## The pattern

The models' own values put honesty and consent first. Halacha puts dignity, peace and honor first, and lets the
literal truth give way to peace. It isn't that AI is more lenient or stricter. It runs on a different moral system.

Kept blind: no Jewish or religious terms in any "what would you do?" question (a Shabbat meal became a dinner party),
and a probe confirmed no model was told who was asking. One answer per question each way; Claude Opus 5.5 graded every
answer against the halachic rubric.
