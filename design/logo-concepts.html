Logo concepts

Three directions for the HalachaBench mark, each shown with the wordmark from the current site, on paper and on dark, at favicon size and as the round X avatar. Pick one direction (or a mix) and it gets refined into final SVG and PNG files.

1. Hei bar

The Hebrew letter ה, for halacha. Its left leg already floats free of the roof; here it becomes a bar from a bar chart, in tekhelet blue. Reads as a letter to anyone who learns, and as a measurement to everyone else.

2. The daf

The shape of a page of Gemara: the main text in the middle, Rashi and Tosafot wrapped around it. Pared down to blocks and lines, it also reads as a table of results, which is what the site is.

3. Interval H

A serif H, the same H as today's favicon, whose crossbar is the site's own score line: an interval with end caps and a blue score dot. It carries the brand you already have and adds the one idea the site is about, measurement with honest error bars.
